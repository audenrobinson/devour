This notice explains how Trinicle Marketing collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit, inquire, become a client or use a future account area.

Trinicle Marketing controls the information described here. Privacy questions and rights requests can be sent to your-email@example.com. Official business contact details will be added when confirmed.

3. How we use it

We use information to answer inquiries, prepare proposals, deliver services, support clients, process payments, secure and improve the website, maintain records and meet legal duties. We do not sell personal information.

4. Lawful bases

We process information where needed to take requested steps or perform a contract, for legitimate interests that do not override your rights, to meet a legal duty, or with consent. Consent may be withdrawn where it is the basis used.

5. Sharing

Necessary information may be shared with trusted hosting, email, CRM, analytics, payment, advertising and professional service providers. It may also be disclosed when legally required or during a business reorganisation. Providers must protect it and use it only for the agreed purpose.

6. International transfers

Some providers may process information outside the UK. Where required, recognised safeguards such as adequacy regulations or approved contractual protections are used.

7. Retention and security

Information is kept only as long as reasonably needed for services, inquiries, accounts, legal records and disputes. Proportionate safeguards are used, but no online system is completely secure. Do not send passwords or payment-card details through the inquiry form.

8. Cookies

Essential storage may be used for security and functionality. If non-essential analytics, advertising or preference cookies are added, suitable information and choices will be provided before use where consent is required.

9. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability or withdrawal of consent. You may complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office. Identity may need to be verified first.

10. Children and updates

Services are intended for businesses, not children. We may update this notice as services, providers or legal duties change; the latest version and date will remain here.
